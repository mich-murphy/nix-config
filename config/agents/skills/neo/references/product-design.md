# Product Design

Decide what should exist before selecting the technical solution.

## Required Decisions

- Who experiences the problem, in what context?
- What evidence shows the current problem or opportunity?
- Which observable user or operator outcome should change?
- What happens now, including workarounds?
- Which success, edge, harm, accessibility, and operational scenarios matter?
- What is explicitly outside scope?
- Which privacy, policy, cost, security, or accessibility constraints apply?
- Which assumptions remain unverified?
- What evidence would accept or disprove the proposal?

Use a prototype as a learning instrument, not an early implementation. Choose
its fidelity from the uncertainty, then return the evidence to product design.

## Gate

Approve product design when representative behavior can be walked through,
outcomes and non-goals are explicit, and assumptions are visible. A feature
list alone is insufficient.

## Evidence Posture

The questions synthesize ISO human-centred design guidance, GOV.UK service
design practice, and repository practitioner reports. The need to validate
product intent has moderate certainty; a fixed interview sequence does not.
