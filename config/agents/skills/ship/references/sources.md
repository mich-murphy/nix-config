# Evidence and Provenance

The workflow is a local-trial synthesis, not a universally proven development
method.

- `software-development-practice-for-agent-steering.md`: lifecycle,
  risk-proportional depth, TDD, refactoring, review, and release-readiness
  synthesis; low-to-moderate certainty.
- `kent-beck-test-driven-development-by-example.md`: TDD mechanics from
  expert demonstration and later implementation reports; moderate certainty
  for the described mechanics, low for general outcome improvement.
- `martin-fowler-refactoring.md` and the installed `$refactor` skill:
  behavior-preserving microsteps and published-interface limits.
- `riccomini-ryaboy-missing-readme.md`: professional lifecycle and code-health
  review guidance.
- `kun-chen-current-workflow.md`: fresh-context review as an inspected
  implementation report; informative, not causal evidence.

Measure acceptance, review rework, escaped defects, later change locality,
human burden, and cost per accepted task. Do not treat prompt adherence,
coverage, lines changed, or pull-request count as quality.
